Camp terms

Booking a camp, fairly.

How places, deposits, payment plans, transfers and refunds work for day camps, two-day camps and retreat camps.

Last updated: October 2026 (draft)

Draft for review by a Philippine lawyer before launch. This text explains how Gising Sining House intends to work. It isn't final, and nothing here is binding until it has been reviewed and published as final.

Your place

A place is yours once we've confirmed it and your deposit is paid. The deposit amount, the balance due date and any payment plan are shown before you pay. Places go in order of confirmation.

Writers aged 16 or 17 need a parent or guardian's written consent before a place is confirmed, and the rules for writers aged 16 and 17 apply at every camp.

Payment plans

Payment plans are due on the dates shown when you book, with everything paid by the balance due date. If a payment is more than 14 days late, your place may be offered to the waitlist, and what you've paid is kept as credit for a future camp.

Transfers

After confirmation the deposit isn't refundable, but you can transfer your place to another writer (we'll check they're a good fit) or move it to a future camp.

If you cancel

  • Before the balance due date: we refund everything except the deposit.
  • After the balance due date: your payments become credit for a future camp.

If we cancel or move a camp

If we have to cancel or move a camp for any reason, including weather, you get a full refund including the deposit, or a transfer to the new dates if you prefer. We're not responsible for flights or other travel costs, so please get travel insurance.

Health, access and food

Tell us about dietary or access needs when you book. We use them only to run your camp, as explained in the privacy policy.

At camp

The community guidelines apply at every camp. We may ask anyone who breaks them seriously to leave, without a refund. We take photos and recordings at camps; tell us if you'd rather not appear in them, and we'll respect that.

Songs written at camp

Songs written at camp follow the member agreement: you keep all of your writer's share, and the publishing is split equally between the writers in the room, 90% in total, with 10% to the house. With three writers that's 30 / 30 / 30 / 10.
