How a brand song works

Brief, writers, demo, final.

  1. 1

    Brief

    Tell us what the song is for, who will hear it, where it will play, and when you need it. We reply with questions and a quote.

  2. 2

    Writers

    We match two or three writers and a producer to the brief: the right genre, language and voice.

  3. 3

    Demo

    You hear a demo and give notes. Changes at this stage are quick and cheap.

  4. 4

    Final

    We produce the finished track and deliver it with stems and the paperwork.

Ownership and licensing

Clear on day one.

Every brand song starts with a written agreement that says who owns the song, what you can do with it, where, and for how long.

Most brands need a licence, not ownership: the right to use the song in a campaign, on certain channels, for a set time. Some want the song outright. Both are possible, and the price reflects which. The writers are always credited and paid properly, and we'll explain every option in plain English before you sign.
