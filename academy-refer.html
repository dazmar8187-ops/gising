Who to refer

Someone with something to say, and a reason they stopped.

You don't need to be sure. If you know someone who was let go from a talent pathway or a music course, left school early, lost work or confidence, or simply never got invited into a room like this, that's enough to start a conversation.

They don't need to play, sing or write already. They don't need to explain their story to us either. We'll start with what they can do today.

16–18s
The Released Class: a full week of writing, study and skills.
Adults
The Foundation Track: plain-English learning, CV and mentoring.
Anyone stuck
The Porch Programme: a steady weekly place to start again.
Not sure?
Choose "not sure yet" and we'll talk it through with them.

What happens next

Five plain steps, no pressure.

We never sign anyone up without their say-so. A referral just opens the door; the person you refer decides whether to walk through it.

Referring someone under 18? Their parent or guardian needs to know, and we'll ask for guardian consent before anything starts.

1

We read it. A real person reads every referral, usually within a week.

2

We get in touch. We contact the person you referred, by the route you've told us they're happy with.

3

A short chat. Online or, from 2027, in Manila. No forms to fill in on the spot, no story to tell.

4

They choose a door. Foundation, Released Class or Porch, or none, and that's fine too.

5

We let you know. Only if they agree. Otherwise we keep their details private, including from you.

Ground rules

Refer with their blessing.

Please do

Before you send it

  • Ask them first. Only refer someone who knows you're doing it and is happy for us to contact them.
  • Keep it short. A few lines on why you think it would help is plenty.
  • Tell us how to reach them. Email, phone or Messenger, whichever they prefer.
Straight talk

What we can and can't do

  • Not an emergency service. If someone is in danger or crisis, contact local emergency services or a crisis line first.
  • Not a clinic. We're a creative community. If someone needs professional care, we'll help them find it and stay alongside them.
  • Private by default. What they tell us stays with us unless they say otherwise.

Questions

Before you refer

Can I refer myself?

Yes. Go to the Academy application instead, it's quicker.

I work for an organisation. Can we refer several people?

Yes. Send one referral now and mention it in the note, and we'll set up a simple way for your organisation to refer people regularly.

Will you tell me how they're getting on?

Only if they agree. Some people love their referrer hearing about it; others want a fresh start. We follow their lead.

Does it cost them anything?

No one is turned away for money. Places are funded by sponsors and partners, and any costs are explained plainly before anyone commits.
