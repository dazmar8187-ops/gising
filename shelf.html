How it works

Three rules for the shelf.

  1. 1

    Signed, or it isn't here

    A song goes on the shelf only when every writer has signed the split. No signed split, no shelf. That protects the writers, and it protects you.

  2. 2

    The writers get it all

    Your money goes to the writers in the shares they signed. The house takes nothing from the shelf. Only the card or wallet fee comes off, and it's shown on every song.

  3. 3

    Your name, if you want

    Tick one box and the writers get your name and email, so they can thank you and tell you when the next song is out. Nobody gets it without that tick.

This is money from fans, paid straight to writers. It's separate from royalties, which are collected through FILSCAP or each writer's own society.
