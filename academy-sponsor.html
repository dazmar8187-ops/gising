What a seat pays for

Everything one writer needs for a term.

A seat is a full term in the Academy for someone who couldn't otherwise afford it. It pays for the sessions, the people and the studio time, so the writer can just show up and work.

We'll send you the current cost of a seat and how payment works when you get in touch. Seats funded during the founding season go to the first cohort.

Weekly sessions
Writing, learning and skills days, online now and in Manila from 2027.
A mentor
One-to-one check-ins on their plan, every month.
Booth time
Studio time to record a finished demo for their portfolio.
Getting there
Help with travel and food for in-person days, where it's the thing standing in the way.
Their songs
Registered under their name. The writer keeps their writer's share.

Our promise to sponsors

Where your money goes, in plain words.

You can count on

What you'll get

  • A plain account. Each term: how many seats were funded, what they cost and what the writers made.
  • Real updates. What your writer worked on, in their words where they're happy to share.
  • An invitation. To the end-of-term Round, where you can hear the songs.
Straight talk

What we won't do

  • Share a writer's details without their permission. Many people come to the Academy for a fresh start.
  • Ask for anything back from them. A sponsored writer owes you nothing, though a thank-you song does turn up sometimes.
  • Promise outcomes. We promise the room, the people and the work. Nobody can promise a hit or a job.

Questions

Before you sponsor

How much does a seat cost?

We're finalising seat costs for the founding cohort. Get in touch and we'll send you the figure and how payment works, with no obligation.

Is it tax-deductible?

Not at the moment. Gising Sining House is being set up as a Philippine company. If that changes, we'll say so plainly on this page.

Can I choose who I sponsor?

You can choose a door (the Released Class, Foundation or Porch), but not a named person. Places go to whoever needs them most.

Can my business be named?

Yes, if you'd like. Cohort sponsors and partners can be thanked by name at the Round and on the site. Individual sponsors can stay anonymous.
