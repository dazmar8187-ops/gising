Reliable, kind, and checked.

What we ask

Before you start

  • A short chat. So we can understand what you'd like to do and match you well.
  • Background checks. Anyone working with 16–18s needs a clearance check (in the Philippines, an NBI clearance) and references.
  • Safeguarding training. Short, required, and provided by us.
  • Our code of conduct. Honest feedback, kindly given, plain English, and no promises we can't keep.
What you get

Why mentors stay

  • Training and support. You won't be thrown in. Darryl and the team are there to back you up.
  • A seat in the Room. Mentors join the online Room as members.
  • The good bit. Watching someone play their first finished song at the Round.

Questions

Before you offer

Do I need to be a professional musician?

No. Teachers, counsellors, accountants and good listeners are just as needed. Tell us what you know and we'll find where it fits.

Is it paid?

Volunteer mentoring isn't paid. Skills leads who run a full module may be paid a session fee, and we'll agree that up front.

Can I mentor from outside the Philippines?

Yes. Most mentoring happens online. In-person sessions in Manila start in 2027.

What if I can only help occasionally?

That's fine. Round listeners and cowrite guests can come when they're free. We'd rather have you sometimes than not at all.
