Community guidelines

The house rules.

Eight rules for the Room, your house tools and every camp. They're short on purpose.

  1. One fair split. Every song written together in the Room or at a camp: the writers keep all of their writer's share, and the publishing is 90% to the writers (split equally) and 10% to the house, which registers and pitches the songs. A three-way cowrite is 30 / 30 / 30 / 10. Songs you write on your own stay 100% yours.
  2. Write splits on the day. Every cowrite ends with a split sheet everyone signs, even for a rough idea. In your house tools it's signed before the first line.
  3. Feedback has a format. One thing that works, one thing to try, one question. Talk about the song, not the person.
  4. Ask before you share. Don't post, release or pitch anyone else's work, voice memo or lyric without their written OK.
  5. No selling in the feed. Share your releases in Wins. Paid services, playlist schemes and “promotion” offers get removed.
  6. Kind and honest, both. Kindness without honesty wastes people's time. Honesty without kindness empties the room.
  7. Show up. Post the prompt, even rough. Reply to two people for every song you post.
  8. Safe room. No harassment, hate or pressure, online or at a camp. Report problems to the hosts; we act on every report, and serious or repeated breaches end a membership.

Younger writers

Some members are 16 or 17. Treat every room as one they might be in. Only the host and mentors can message an under-18 member privately in the house. Outside it, adults don't contact an under-18 member privately, and never ask to meet one alone. The full rules are in the terms.

Reporting a problem

Use the report button in the Room or your house tools, or write to the house email (to be added). Tell us what happened and where. The hosts read every report, act on it, and keep it confidential.

See also the terms of membership and the member agreement.
