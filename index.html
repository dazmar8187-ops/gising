What this is

A house you can write in. And a room that comes with you.

Most songwriters write alone, finish too little, and never hear an honest word about the song until a stranger skips it. That isn't a talent problem. It's a room problem.

Gising Sining House fixes the room. You get a weekly prompt, a cowrite partner, a feedback circle that listens properly, and, a few times a year, a physical house where you write for days with people who are as serious about it as you are.

Nobody promises you a hit here. You'll write more, finish more, and know more people who can help you get the songs heard.

Camps

Twelve writers. Songs you didn't know you had.

Each camp runs on cowrite rotations. Every morning you're matched with a new pair of writers and a producer. By evening you've finished a song and recorded a rough demo. On the last night we play everything back in front of an audience.

Twelve places per camp, three ways to do it. Day camps welcome every level. Two-day and retreat camps suit writers who have finished at least a handful of songs.

Why I'm building this

The song gets better when someone else is in the room.

Good writers stall for the same reason, again and again. They're alone with the work, and nobody tells them the truth about it or about the money.

Gising Sining House is the room that fixes that. People who write every week. Honest notes. And the business side explained plainly, so the songs you finish here can work for you for years.

We start online, now, with the Room. The Manila house opens in 2027, because the music there deserves a bigger stage. Then Bali, where writers from both sides of the Pacific can meet halfway.

Questions

Before you join

Do I need to be an experienced songwriter?

No. Day camps and the Online Room are built for people who are early. What matters is that you keep showing up and finish things.

Who owns the songs I write here?

You keep all of your writer's share, the royalties paid straight to songwriters. Songs written together in the Room or at a camp share the publishing 90% to the writers and 10% to the house, so a three-way cowrite is 30 / 30 / 30 / 10. The house's 10% pays for registering and pitching the songs. Songs you write on your own stay 100% yours.

I don't live in Manila. Is it still worth it?

Yes. The Online Room runs every day across time zones, and camps are built for people who travel in. We'll help with where to stay.

Will this get my songs placed or signed?

Nobody can promise that, and you should be wary of anyone who does. You'll write more, finish more, and meet people who work in the industry. What you do with that is yours.

When does it open?

The online room is open now for its founding season, free. The Manila house follows in 2027. Founding members hear first about camps.

What happens when the founding season ends?

You'll get an email before anything changes, and you can leave at any time with everything you wrote. No card is needed to join.
