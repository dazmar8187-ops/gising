How it works

Made in Manila, made to order, and the writer gets paid.

  1. 1

    Printed here

    Every piece is printed by a small Manila print shop, to order. Nothing sits in a warehouse and nothing gets thrown away.

  2. 2

    Credited on the tag

    When a design carries a member's line, the tag says who wrote it and which song it's from.

  3. 3

    Paid on every piece

    The writer gets a share of every piece sold with their line on it. They keep the copyright; the house only gets permission for that one design.

  4. 4

    The rest sends a writer

    What's left after printing, shipping and the writer's share goes to scholarship places.
