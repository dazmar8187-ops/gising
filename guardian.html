Parent or guardian consent

Your consent for a young writer.

A writer aged 16 or 17 has asked to join Gising Sining House and gave your email as their parent or guardian. Nothing starts until you agree.

What you're agreeing to

  • Safeguarding. In-person sessions for under-18s always have more than one adult present and a trained safeguarding lead. Online one-to-one mentoring happens on house video calls, and you can join any session.
  • Checked adults. Mentors who work with under-18s have a background check, references and safeguarding training.
  • Private messages. Your child can message other under-18s, the host and mentors. Other adults can't message them privately, and the host can read any conversation with an under-18 in it.
  • Privacy. Your child's profile is never public, they aren't shown on the member map, and we don't share their photos or recordings outside the house without your consent.
  • Their songs. Your child keeps all of their writer's share. You co-sign the member agreement and any split sheet with them, because under-18s can't sign agreements on their own.
  • You stay in control. You can see, correct or delete their information, or withdraw consent, at any time by emailing us.

The full rules are in the terms, the privacy policy and the member agreement.
