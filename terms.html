Terms of membership

The deal, in plain English.

What you get, what we ask of you, and how membership works before and after the founding season.

Last updated: October 2026 (draft)

Draft for review by a Philippine lawyer before launch. This text explains how Gising Sining House intends to work. It isn't final, and nothing here is binding until it has been reviewed and published as final.

Who these terms are with

These terms are between you and Gising Sining House, a songwriting community run from the Philippines and being set up as a Philippine company. The host is Darryl Saunders. Contact: the house email (to be added).

The founding season

The online Room is free during the founding season, which runs until 31 March 2027. No card is needed to join.

Before the founding season ends we'll email you with the price, the date it starts and how to leave. You'll get at least 30 days' notice, and nobody is charged without agreeing first.

Your membership

  • Membership is for one person. Use your real name and keep your sign-in link to yourself.
  • You need to be 18 or over, or 16 or 17 with a parent or guardian's consent (see writers aged 16 and 17). We don't accept members under 16.
  • The Room (the feed, channels, calendar and courses) runs on GoHighLevel Communities. Your house tools (the Pairing, writing rooms, ownership forms, the 5 Keys and the Pro Desk) run on this website.
  • The Manila house opens in 2027. Until then, membership is the online Room and your house tools.

How we treat each other

Everyone in the house follows the community guidelines: kind and honest notes, ask before you share someone else's work, no selling in the feed, and no harassment, hate or pressure. We act on every report.

Your songs

You keep all of your writer's share of every song you write here.

Songs written together in the Room or at a camp are shared the house way: the publishing is 90% to the writers, split equally unless you all agree otherwise before you start, and 10% to the house, which registers and pitches the songs. A three-way cowrite is 30 / 30 / 30 / 10. Songs you write on your own stay 100% yours. The details are in the member agreement.

What you post

You keep the rights to what you post. You give the house permission to show it to members inside the Room and your house tools, and nothing more. Don't post anything you don't have the right to share.

Notes you give and receive are for the writer to use freely.

Writers aged 16 and 17

We welcome writers aged 16 and 17 with a parent or guardian's consent. We don't accept members under 16.

  • Consent first. When a 16- or 17-year-old joins or applies, we email their parent or guardian. Nothing starts, including access to the Room, until the parent or guardian has given consent in writing.
  • Agreements are co-signed. Because under-18s can't enter binding agreements on their own, a parent or guardian signs the member agreement and every split sheet alongside them. The song is still the young writer's, and so is their share.
  • Safeguarding. In-person sessions for under-18s always have more than one adult present and a trained safeguarding lead. Online one-to-one mentoring with an under-18 happens on house video calls, and the parent or guardian can join any session.
  • Checked adults. Mentors and volunteers who work with under-18s have a background check (in the Philippines, an NBI clearance), references and safeguarding training.
  • Contact. In the house, an under-18 member can message other under-18s, the host and mentors; other adult members can't message them privately. The host, as safeguarding lead, can read any private conversation with an under-18 in it. Outside the house, adult members must not contact an under-18 member privately or ask to meet them alone. We treat a breach as serious.
  • Privacy. Under-18 profiles are never public, and we don't share their photos or recordings outside the house without their parent or guardian's consent.
  • Concerns. Anyone can raise a concern with the safeguarding lead at the house email (to be added). We act on every one, and report to the authorities where the law requires.

Members turn 18 into full membership automatically. Agreements signed while they were under 18 stay in place, and they can ask us to review anything they signed with their guardian.

Camps and services

Camps follow the camp terms. Services (reviews, mentor sessions, cowrites with a pro, demos and registration help) have their own price and terms, shown before you pay.

Second Chance Academy

Second Chance Academy is the part of Gising Sining House for people who were dropped, left behind or never got in the room. These terms apply to it, with the following additions.

  • Places. Applying doesn't guarantee a place. We offer places to the founding cohort as the programme takes shape, and programme details may change before the Manila house opens in 2027.
  • Cost. No one is turned away for money. Any cost is set out in writing before you commit, and you can tell us what you can contribute, including nothing.
  • The Released Class is for writers aged 16 to 18. Writers aged 16 or 17 follow the rules for writers aged 16 and 17.
  • Your songs. Songs written in the Academy follow the same rules as the rest of the house and the member agreement.
  • Not therapy, not an emergency service. The Academy is a creative, practical community. It isn't medical, psychological or crisis care. If you're in danger or crisis, contact local emergency services first.
  • No qualifications yet. The Academy doesn't currently award certificates or qualifications.
  • Referrals. Only refer someone who knows and agrees. We contact them once; if they don't want to go ahead, we delete the referral.
  • Sponsors. The cost of a seat and how payment works are agreed in writing before you pay. Sponsorship pays for a place; it gives no rights over the writer, their songs or their information, and sponsored writers owe sponsors nothing. Sponsorship isn't currently tax-deductible.
  • Mentors and volunteers agree to our code of conduct and safeguarding rules, and anyone working with under-18s completes a background check first. Volunteering is unpaid unless a fee is agreed in writing.
  • Leaving. You can leave the Academy at any time. We can end a place for serious or repeated breaches of the community guidelines or safeguarding rules.

What we can and can't promise

We promise the room, the schedule, the notes and the work we describe. Nobody can promise a placement, a deal or an income, and we don't. To the extent the law allows, the house isn't liable for indirect losses, and our total liability is limited to what you've paid us in the previous 12 months.

Leaving

You can leave at any time from your account or by emailing us. Songs already written together keep the split you signed. We can suspend or end a membership for serious or repeated breaches of the community guidelines; except in serious cases, we'll warn you first.

Changes and law

If we change these terms in a way that matters, we'll email members at least 30 days before. These terms are governed by the laws of the Philippines.
