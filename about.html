About the house

Who runs Gising Sining House.

Gising Sining means art that awakens. A songwriting house for writers who want to finish songs with other people. It starts online now, with the Manila house opening in 2027.

Host

Darryl Saunders

I host the Room, the Pairing and the camps, and I'm the person you'll hear from most. If something isn't working for you, tell me.

Darryl's full bio and photo are coming soon.

Visiting mentor

Paul K Saunders

Paul has spent about forty years in music: pirate radio, the BBC, major-label A&R, festivals, management and his own label. He joins the house as a visiting mentor and leads the money-side workshops on splits, publishing and getting paid.

Why Manila

The music there deserves a bigger stage.

Some of the most musical people on earth, a P-pop and OPM scene that's growing fast, and an English-speaking writing culture that already crosses borders. Manila is a natural home base for writers across Asia, and close enough to Bali, Jakarta and Sydney to bring them together.

Gising Sining House is being set up as a Philippine company, with Filipino partners holding the majority.

What we promise

Five things you can hold us to.

  1. Your writer's share is yours. The house never takes any of it.
  2. One fair split, up front. Songs written together share the publishing 90% to the writers and 10% to the house, signed before you write.
  3. Notice before any charge. The founding season is free, and you'll hear from us at least 30 days before anything costs money.
  4. Honest notes. Kind and honest, in a format you can use.
  5. No promises we can't keep. Nobody can promise a hit. We promise the room, the people and the work.
