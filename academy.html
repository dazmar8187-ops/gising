Everyone gets a second take.

What this is

A second take, not a lesser one.

Second Chance Academy runs inside Gising Sining House, on the same rooms, the same writers and the same honest feedback. It adds the things people often missed the first time round: learning in plain English, practical life skills, mentoring, and a route to what comes next.

Songwriting is the way in, not the only point. Along the way the Academy rebuilds the everyday things that hold a life together: reading and writing with confidence, a CV you're proud of, money you understand, and people who have your back.

Nobody here promises you a deal, a job or a hit, and you should be wary of anyone who does.

Three ways in

One house. Three doors.

Pick the one that sounds most like where you are right now. You can move between them.

The Foundation Track

Adults any level, self-paced

For adults who missed the basics the first time round, or lost them since. Plain English, short sessions, no classroom pressure.

  • Bite-sized lessons you can finish in under an hour
  • Reading, writing and speaking with confidence, practised through lyrics and stories
  • CV, applications and interviews that sound like you
  • One-to-one mentoring, in person or online
Start the Foundation Track

The Released Class

16–18 full-week programme

For young people let go from a talent pathway, a youth programme or a music course, or who left school early. Structured, serious, and built around you.

  • Weekly writing, rehearsal and performance, with a showcase every Friday
  • Study time built in, so the learning never gets skipped
  • Vocational modules: live sound, studio assistance, music facilitation
  • A clear next step at the end: study, work, or your own path
Apply to the Released Class

The Porch Programme

Everyone drop-in, weekly

For people who feel stuck because life piled on: work gone, confidence gone, support gone. A steady place to start moving again, one small win at a time.

  • Wellbeing, connection and creative time, every week
  • Money skills for real life: budgeting, splits, getting paid
  • Introductions to local services, training and self-employment routes
  • A small circle of people who know your name

How a second chance works · E G B D F

Five lines on the stave. Five steps back up.

The Academy climbs the same five lines of the stave as the rest of Gising Sining House, and starts, like everyone, in the Living Room. Each line is a step in getting your feet back under you.

E

Restart

One prompt, one page, one honest line. We begin with what you can do today and build a routine around it. You also sort the practical things here: your goals, your learning plan, your first CV.

The Desk
G

Reconnect

You're matched with a writing partner and a mentor. Making something with another person is the quickest way back into the world, and nobody has to tell their story first.

The Cowrite Room
B

Be heard

Share what you've made in a room that wants you to do well. Small circles first, then a proper listening night. Confidence comes back when you watch people lean in.

The Round
D

Keep proof

Record a demo that carries the song. A finished recording shows you started something and finished it. It goes straight into your portfolio.

The Booth
F

Move forward

The honest talk about money, work, study and what happens next. You leave with a plan that names your next three steps and the person who'll check in on them.

The Porch

What a week looks like

Show up Monday. Play Friday.

Every track shares the same weekly shape, so the rhythm stays simple. The Released Class does the full week. Foundation and Porch members choose the days that suit them.

Writers aged 16 or 17 need a parent or guardian's consent. Under-18 sessions always have more than one adult present and a trained safeguarding lead. See the rules for writers aged 16 and 17.

Mon

Prompt and plan. A new prompt, a short learning session, and one goal for the week.

Tue

Cowrite. Paired with a writer and a mentor. Make something by the end of the day.

Wed

Skills day. A practical module: live sound, studio basics, facilitation, or writing for work.

Thu

Money and life. Budgeting, splits, getting paid, and a one-to-one check-in with your mentor.

Fri

The Round. A listening night. You play what you made, and everyone claps for the same reason.

The house rules

How we treat each other.

In the room

What you can count on

The Academy runs on a few plain promises.

  • No shame. You don't owe anyone an explanation for take one.
  • Honest feedback, kindly given. We tell the truth about the work, as people who want you to succeed.
  • Plain English. If a word or a contract clause needs explaining, we explain it.
  • Your songs stay yours. The same writer's-share rules as the rest of Gising Sining House, written down before you start.
Straight talk

What we are, and aren't

We'd rather be clear about the limits than overpromise.

  • Safe by design. Programmes for young people run with trained safeguarding leads, guardian consent, and more than one adult in every session.
  • Support, not a substitute. We're a creative community, not a clinic. When someone needs professional help, we'll help them find it and stay alongside them.

Questions

Before you apply

Do I need to be able to play or write already?

No. Plenty of people start with nothing but something to say. The Foundation Track and the Porch Programme are built for beginners, and the Released Class welcomes every level.

Is it free?

Nobody gets turned away for money. Seats are supported by sponsors and partners, and you tell us what you can contribute, which can be nothing. We'll set out any costs plainly before you commit.

Where does it happen?

Online from the founding season, so you can join from anywhere. In person in Manila from 2027, once the house opens. Referrals from other places are welcome and we'll talk through what's possible.

Is this therapy?

No. It's a creative, practical community. Many people find that making things with others helps them feel better, but we're not a replacement for professional mental health care, and we'll always point you to local services if you need them.

I'm under 18. Can I join?

Yes, if you're 16 or 17, with a parent or guardian's consent. We'll email them before anything starts, and they co-sign any agreement with you. Sessions for under-18s always have more than one adult present and a trained safeguarding lead. The full rules are in the terms. We can't accept anyone under 16 yet.
