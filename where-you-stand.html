Where You Stand Right Now · free · about ten minutes

Where do your songs actually stand?

This isn't a personality test, and it won't tell you who you are. It's twenty honest statements about your songwriting, four on each line of the stave, and five about how you work in a writing room. You get the line you're strong on, the line that's open, your role in a room, and one thing to do this week.

Find out where you standWhat are the 5 Keys?

Where you stand, not who you are

A type says you're wired a certain way and leaves you there. This says where you are today, and today changes. Come back in three months and you should see lines move.

What the five lines are

  • E · Establish: finished songs, signed splits, registered work
  • G · Grow: writing with people and getting better from it
  • B · Book: songs heard by people in a room
  • D · Deliver: taking a song all the way to a pitchable demo
  • F · Flourish: the money side, and a rhythm that lasts
