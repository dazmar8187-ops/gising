Privacy policy

Your information, plainly.

What Gising Sining House collects when you join, book or sign up, why, who helps us handle it, and the rights you have over it.

Last updated: October 2026 (draft)

Who we are

Gising Sining House is a songwriting community and house, run from the Philippines. It is being set up as a Philippine company. Until that company is registered, the house's host, Darryl Saunders, is responsible for your information. You can reach us at the house email (to be added).

We follow the Philippines Data Privacy Act of 2012 (Republic Act 10173) and its rules. If you live in the European Union or the United Kingdom, the GDPR also gives you the rights described below.

What we collect

  • When you join: your first name, email, the country you live in, where you are as a writer, what you bring to a room, which parts of the house interest you, and your consent to emails and to the member agreement (with the version you agreed to).
  • When you reserve a camp place: the above, plus a link to one of your songs, your pod and room preference, and any dietary or access needs you tell us.
  • When you book or ask about a service: your name, email, the service, any song link and notes you add.
  • The weekly prompt email: your first name and email.
  • The contact form: your name, email and message.
  • Second Chance Academy applications: your first name, email, where you live, which programme you're applying for, what you tell us about where you are now, and, for the Released Class, your age and a parent or guardian's email.
  • Academy referrals: the referrer's name, email, role and organisation, and the first name, contact details, age range and circumstances of the person they refer. The referrer confirms that person knows and agrees.
  • Academy sponsors: your name, email, organisation, what you'd like to fund, and whether you want to stay anonymous.
  • Academy mentors and volunteers: your name, email, where you live, skills, availability, and, if you'll work with under-18s, your background check (such as an NBI clearance) and references.
  • In the Room and your house tools: your profile (photo, city, languages, styles, a short bio, a link to your music), what you post, the notes you give, your writing rooms and lyric sheets, signed split sheets, and songs you send to the Pro Desk.
  • Technical information: if analytics is switched on, we use Plausible, which counts visits without cookies and without identifying you. The members' app keeps some settings in your browser so it works between visits.

Dietary and access needs can say something about your health. We treat them as sensitive personal information, ask for your consent, and use them only to run your camp.

What people tell us in Academy applications and referrals can include personal circumstances such as health, education, work or family. We treat it as sensitive personal information: only the people running the Academy see it, we use it only to offer and run a place, and we never share it with sponsors. Background checks are also sensitive and are seen only by the people who approve mentors.

Why we use it

  • To run your membership, the Room, your house tools, camps and services you ask for (this is needed to provide what you signed up for).
  • To send the emails you asked for: your invite, the weekly prompt, Pairing groups, notes on your songs. You can unsubscribe from any of them.
  • To keep accurate split sheets and song records, register songs with FILSCAP or your home society when the member agreement applies, and pay out what songs earn.
  • To keep the community safe and act on reports.
  • To understand how the site is used and improve it.
  • To meet our legal and tax obligations.

Who helps us handle it

We don't sell your information. These services handle it on our behalf, under contracts that require them to protect it:

  • HighLevel (GoHighLevel): our members' database, email and the Room community.
  • Netlify: hosts this website and the small server functions behind your house tools.
  • Plausible Analytics: visit counts, if switched on.
  • Payment providers (such as Stripe and PayMongo for cards, GCash and Maya), once payments open. We never see your full card details.
  • Video call services (such as Zoom or Google Meet) for live sessions.

Some of these companies store information outside the Philippines, including in the United States. Where that happens we rely on their contractual safeguards.

Inside the house, your cowriters see your name and share on the split sheets you sign together, and other members see your profile and what you post in the Room. When a song you wrote together is registered or pitched, we share the songwriter details the collecting society or the buyer needs.

How long we keep it

  • Your profile and Room activity: while you're a member, then deleted or anonymised within 12 months of leaving, unless you ask sooner.
  • Split sheets and song records: for as long as the song can earn money, because they decide who gets paid.
  • Payment and accounting records: as long as Philippine law requires.
  • Email sign-ups: until you unsubscribe.
  • Academy applications: while you're in the Academy, then 12 months; applications that don't go ahead are deleted after 12 months.
  • Referrals: deleted within 60 days if the person referred doesn't want to go ahead or doesn't reply.
  • Background checks: while you mentor with us, then deleted within 12 months.
  • Parent and guardian consent records: until the writer turns 18, then for as long as the agreements they co-signed apply.

Your rights

Under the Data Privacy Act you have the right to be informed, to access your information, to object, to have it corrected, erased or blocked, to data portability, to be indemnified for damage, and to complain to the National Privacy Commission (privacy.gov.ph). If you're in the EU or UK, you also have the GDPR rights to restrict processing and to complain to your local data protection authority.

To use any of these rights, write to the house email (to be added). We reply within 30 days.

Keeping it safe

Access to member information is limited to the people who run the house. Your house tools use a secure sign-in link sent to your email, and the connection to this site is encrypted. If something goes wrong that puts your information at risk, we'll tell you and the National Privacy Commission as the law requires.

Writers aged 16 and 17

We accept writers aged 16 and 17 with a parent or guardian's consent, and don't accept anyone under 16. If we learn that someone under 16 has signed up, we delete their information.

  • We ask for a parent or guardian's email and contact them for consent before the young writer gets access to anything. We keep a record of that consent.
  • We collect only what we need to run their membership or Academy place.
  • Under-18 profiles are never public, they aren't shown on the member map, and we don't share their photos, recordings or details outside the house without their parent or guardian's consent.
  • The host, as safeguarding lead, can read private messages in any conversation that includes an under-18 member.
  • A parent or guardian can ask to see, correct or delete their child's information, and can withdraw consent at any time.
  • If we have a safeguarding concern, we may share information with the parent or guardian or the authorities where the law allows or requires it.

Changes and contact

If we change this policy in a way that matters, we'll email members before it takes effect. Questions: the house email (to be added).
