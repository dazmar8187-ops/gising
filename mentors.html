Mentors

People who know, in the room with you.

Visiting mentors bring working experience from the music business into the house: notes on your songs, sessions on your plan, and briefs from the markets you want to write for.

PKS

Visiting mentor · the money side

Paul K Saunders

About forty years in music: pirate radio, the BBC, major-label A&R, festivals, management and his own label. He's watched good writers stall from every side of the desk, usually for want of honest notes and a plain explanation of the money.

  • Leads the Money Side workshop at Camp 01 in Tagaytay
  • Takes Mentor Sessions on splits, publishing, deals and getting paid
  • Writes the Money Side lessons in the Room

More mentors are announced to members in January 2027: writers, producers and people who buy songs, across the markets the Circles write for.

What mentors do

Four ways a mentor helps.

The Pro Desk

Every member puts one song a month in front of a mentor and gets written notes: one thing that works, one thing to try, one question.

Circles

Small rooms of up to twelve writers led by a mentor who works in that market, with a real brief each month and a pitch when a song is ready.

Sessions

One-to-one Mentor Sessions and video Song Reviews, booked through Services.

Camps

Mentors join the camps for notes, workshops and the listening night.

Founding season

Get your first song in front of a mentor.
