Scholarships · sponsored places

Talent isn't the thing that's scarce.

Some of the best writers in the Philippines will never pay for a camp. That says nothing about their songs. Scholarship places put them in the room anyway: a sponsored seat at a camp, or a sponsored year in the Room, with everything every other writer gets.

Apply for a placeSponsor a writer

Who it's for

Filipino writers with songs and no budget.

You can apply if

  • You live in the Philippines
  • You've written songs, finished or not, and can share one
  • Paying for a camp or a membership would stop you coming
  • You'll show up: to the prompt, the Pairing, and the other writers

What a place includes

  • Camp place: a seat at a day, two-day or retreat camp, with everything the other writers get. For retreats, that includes room and meals.
  • Room place: a year in the online Room once the founding season ends.
  • Travel help for retreat camps, where a sponsor covers it.

Places are limited and we can't offer one to everyone who applies. Nobody is asked to prove they're poor: tell us your situation in your own words.

How it works

Four steps, no essays.

  1. 1

    Apply

    Fill in the short form below and share one song. A voice memo is fine.

  2. 2

    We listen

    I listen to every song myself, with a mentor from the house.

  3. 3

    We match

    Places go to writers whose songs and situation fit the camp or season a sponsor is funding.

  4. 4

    You're in

    You join like any other writer. Nobody else in the room knows who's on a scholarship unless you tell them.

Where the money comes from

Sponsors now. Members later.

For now, places are funded by sponsors: companies, organisations and people who want more Filipino songs written and heard.

Later, part of what members in higher-income countries pay will go into scholarship places too, so the room stays open to the writers it was built for.

For companies and organisations

Sponsor a writer

Fund a camp place or a year in the Room for a Filipino songwriter. You'll hear about the songs your place helped write, with the writer's permission. We'll agree how you're credited before anything is announced.

Talk to us about sponsoring

What sponsors don't get

Any share of the songs. Scholarship writers own their work exactly like everyone else: all of their writer's share, and the same 30 / 30 / 30 / 10 publishing split on songs written together in the house.
