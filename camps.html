How our camps work

New people every morning. A finished song every night.

A camp is the fastest way I know to get better at writing songs. You stop waiting for the right mood. You walk into a room with two writers you met yesterday and a producer, and by evening there's a song with a beginning, a middle and an end, and a demo you can play to people.

Then you do it again the next day with different people. By the end of the week you've written three songs, with nine writers, in three styles you might never have tried alone. You'll also know exactly what you bring to a room.

Every song gets a signed split sheet on the day it's written, so every writer leaves with their share in writing, and with the demos.

Who it's for

Writers who finish things, or want to.

  • You've finished at least three songs, however rough
  • You can play an instrument, sing a melody, write a lyric, or make a beat. One is enough
  • You want honest notes, and you'll give them kindly
  • You're curious about the business side, not scared of it

Not sure you're ready? Apply anyway and tell me where you are. If this camp isn't right yet, I'll tell you which one is.

Who you'll work with

Hosted by me, with producers and mentors from the house.

I'm Darryl Saunders, and I'll host the week. Visiting mentor Paul K Saunders, who has spent about forty years in music (pirate radio, the BBC, major-label A&R, festivals, management and his own label), runs the money-side workshop.

Four producers and two guest mentors join us. Their names are announced to applicants in January 2027.

Practical things

Getting there, what to bring, and the small print.

Getting there

The shuttle leaves a central pickup point in Metro Manila at 11:00 on your pod's Wednesday and brings you back by about 15:30 on Sunday.

Flying in? Land at Manila (NAIA) by the Tuesday before your pod and we'll send hotels near the pickup. Many nationalities can enter the Philippines visa-free for up to 30 days. Check the rules for your passport.

What to bring

  • Your instrument, if you play one
  • A laptop with your DAW, if you produce
  • Headphones, and a notebook
  • A phone for voice memos
  • A light jacket for the evenings
  • Two or three song ideas you haven't finished

Terms

  • Payment and refund terms are sent with your confirmation, before you pay anything.
  • If we have to cancel or move the camp for any reason, including weather, you get a full refund, or a transfer if you prefer.
  • Songs written at camp are shared the house way: you keep all of your writer's share, and the publishing is split 90% between the writers in the room and 10% to the house, which pays for registering and pitching the songs.

Questions

Before you apply

Why do I have to apply? Can't I just buy a place?

A camp only works if every room has people who can finish a song together. The application takes five minutes and helps me build good groups. Most people who apply honestly get in.

I'm not a founding member yet. Can I still get a founding place?

Yes. The join and reserve form makes you a founding member of the online room (free) and asks for your camp place in one go.

Who owns the songs written at camp?

The people who wrote them, recorded on a split sheet signed on the day. You keep all of your writer's share. The publishing is split equally between the writers in the room, 90% in total, with 10% to the house. With three writers that's 30 / 30 / 30 / 10. The house's 10% pays for registering the songs and pitching the best ones to artists, brands and sync.

Will there be industry people at the listening night?

We invite people from the Manila music community, which can include artists, producers and people from labels and publishers. I won't promise who comes, or that anyone signs anything. The night is for playing the songs well.

What if a typhoon hits?

March is dry season and storms are rare, but if we ever have to move or cancel the camp you get a full refund, or a transfer to the new dates.
