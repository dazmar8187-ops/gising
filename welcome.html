Welcome to the room.

Here's how to start this week. It takes about fifteen minutes, and the people who do all four are the ones still writing here in six months.

Accept your invite

Check your email for an invite to the community and set your password. If it isn't there in five minutes, look in spam or Promotions.

Open the RoomOpen your house tools

The Room (the feed, channels, calendar and lessons) runs in the community app. Your house tools (the Pairing, writing rooms, ownership forms, the 5 Keys and the Pro Desk) are on this site.

Put the app on your phone

Prompts, feedback and cowrite requests arrive as notifications. The room works best in your pocket.

iPhone app Android app

Introduce yourself

Post in Introductions. Copy this and fill in the blanks:

Hi, I'm ___ from ___. I write ___ (lyrics / melody / production / all of it). A song I wish I'd written: ___ What I want from this room: ___ Looking for cowriters who ___
Copied.

Answer one other person

Find someone else's introduction and reply with one honest, kind sentence. That's how the room starts working.

This week's prompt · 01

The room you grew up in

Write a verse and a chorus set in one room of the home you grew up in. Name three real objects in it. Don't say how you feel; let the objects say it.

Post a voice memo or the words in the Weekly Prompt channel by Sunday. Rough is fine. Finished beats perfect.
